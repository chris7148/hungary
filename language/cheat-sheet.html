Language & Pronunciation Guidance

• Stress is always on the 1st syllable of every word.
SZ = 's' (see)
S = 'sh' (shoe)
C = 'ts' (cats)
CS = 'ch' (chair)
GY = 'dy' (soft d)
J / LY = 'y' (yes)
A = short 'o' (lot)
Á = long 'ah' (father)
